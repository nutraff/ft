What a cookie is

A cookie is a small text file a website stores in your browser. It remembers you across pages so the site works correctly, measures traffic, and — in the case of affiliate links — attributes a purchase back to the site that sent you there.

Opting out

  • Analytics cookies: Use Google's Google Analytics opt-out add-on or, for a privacy-friendly alternative, your browser's tracking-prevention settings. You may also enable "Do Not Track" in your browser; we honor it where the tool supports it.
  • Affiliate cookies: These are placed on the retailer's domain (for example amazon.com), not on our site. To avoid them, simply do not click our product links and purchase directly from the retailer's site instead. Note that if you choose this route, we will not earn a commission and you are helping no one fund the site.
  • All cookies: You can clear, block, or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time. See your browser's Help section. Clearing strictly-necessary cookies may degrade site function.

What we do not do

  • We do not use cookies for advertising, retargeting, or building a personal profile about you.
  • We do not sell cookies or cookie data to third parties.
  • We do not track individual users across sessions beyond what is technically necessary to keep the site usable.
