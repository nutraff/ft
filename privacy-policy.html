Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information FurTrouble ("we," "us") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to every page on the site, including all our pet-care posts. Please read it before using the site. Last updated: October 5, 2026.

1. Information we collect

a. Affiliate and advertising data

When you click a product link on our site (for example to Amazon or Chewy), the retailer may use cookies, pixels, or other tracking technologies to recognize that you came from us and to attribute any purchase to us as the affiliate. We do not control those third-party technologies; each retailer's own privacy policy governs the data they collect. Our links are intended to help you purchase products you already chose, and we may earn a commission on those purchases.

b. Analytics

We may use an analytics tool (such as Google Analytics or a privacy-friendly equivalent) to understand aggregate traffic: which posts are read, average visit length, and where visitors come from. This data is anonymized where the tool allows. Analytics data is not shared with advertisers.

c. Contact information you send us

If you email us with a question, a product correction, or feedback, we receive and store the email address you use to contact us. We use it only to respond to you. We do not sell it, build a marketing list from it, or share it with third parties.

d. Images and on-page content

Our posts contain images licensed from Pixabay and product images/data pulled from public retailer pages. We do not collect or store information about you through on-page content.

1b. Email marketing

If you subscribe to our email list, we collect your email address only. Emails are managed by a third-party provider (Buttondown / Kit / MailerLite), whose own privacy policy governs their handling of the address. We send a weekly deals newsletter; every email carries a one-click unsubscribe link. We do not sell or share email addresses, and unsubscribe requests are honored immediately (within 24 hours).

2. How we use the information

  • To operate and improve the site (which topics to write about next, which product picks to refresh).
  • To respond to your direct email inquiries.
  • To comply with legal obligations and to protect against fraud.

3. Cookies

We use a small number of cookies: strictly necessary cookies for site function, analytics cookies to measure traffic, and affiliate-link cookies placed by the retailers our links point to. See our Cookie Policy for details and how to opt out. You can also manage or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it only with:

  • Our hosting provider, to keep the site up.
  • Analytics and affiliate-link networks, in the limited ways described above.
  • Law enforcement, if legally required.

5. Your rights (U.S. and California)

If you are a U.S. resident, and specifically if you reside in California, you have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), including the right to know what personal information we hold, to request deletion, and to opt out of "selling" or "sharing" personal information. Because we do not sell personal information, the opt-out is largely theoretical, but you may exercise any of these rights by emailing contact@furtrouble.com. We will respond within the statutory window (typically 45 days). We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors under 16; if you are a parent of a minor who contacted us, email us and we will delete their data.

6. Data retention

Analytics data is retained for as long as the tool's default retention setting (typically 26 months) or until we change it. Emails you send us are kept for 90 days and then deleted unless needed for ongoing support.

7. Security

We use HTTPS across the site. We do not ask you to create an account or log in, so we do not store passwords or authentication tokens. If you believe you have found a security issue, contact us and we will investigate.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

For any privacy question, request, or complaint, email contact@furtrouble.com with the subject line "Privacy." For California residents who are not satisfied with our response, you may also contact the California Privacy Protection Agency.
